Welcome

Shree Reda Samadhi Temple is a holy pilgrimage site located in Shrikhetra Aale. Shree Sant Dnyaneshwar Maharaj, together with Nivruttinath, Sopandev, and Muktabai, established the samadhi of the Reda here. This sacred place in the Warkari tradition is renowned for devotion, faith, and spiritual practice.

Festivals

Information about major festivals and religious traditions at the temple.

Maha shivratri

Mahashivratri includes a week-long Harinam Saptah and a flower-shower ceremony at the Samadhi at 12:30 PM.

Read more →

Chaitra Jatra

A three-day festival from Chaitra Vadya Ekadashi to Trayodashi featuring kirtans, wrestling matches (Kusti), and the breaking of a Dahi Handi.

Read more →

Monthly observances

Nearby villages take turns managing Annadan and kirtans on Ekadashi and Dwadashi, continuing the Warkari tradition of seva and community participation.
