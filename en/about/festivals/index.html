Festivals

Major annual festivals and traditions at the temple.

Maha shivratri

Mahashivratri includes a week-long Harinam Saptah and a flower-shower ceremony at the Samadhi at 12:30 PM.

Read more →

Chaitra Jatra

A three-day festival from Chaitra Vadya Ekadashi to Trayodashi featuring kirtans, wrestling matches (Kusti), and the breaking of a Dahi Handi.

Read more →

Monthly observances

Nearby villages take turns managing Annadan and kirtans on Ekadashi and Dwadashi, continuing the Warkari tradition of seva and community participation.
